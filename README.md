# 📋 HR & Finance Excel Templates

> **Purpose:** A collection of production-ready Excel templates for HR administration and financial management, built and deployed in real organisational settings across Nigeria.

---

## 🗂 Repository Overview

This repository contains professionally designed Excel templates covering payroll processing, tax computation, fleet management, and employee leave tracking. Each template has been used in live production environments and incorporates Nigerian statutory compliance requirements (PAYE, Pension, NHIS, NSITF).

| Template | Function | Compliance |
|---|---|---|
| PAYE Tax Template | Personal Income Tax computation | FIRS / State IRS |
| Payroll Template | Full payslip generation | Pension, NHIS, NSITF |
| Vehicle Fueling Schedule | Fleet fuel expense tracking | Internal audit ready |
| Employee Leave Tracker | Annual leave management | Nigerian Labour Act |

### 1. PAYE Tax Computation Template
A structured template for computing Personal Income Tax (PIT) in line with Nigerian tax legislation. Incorporates:
- **Progressive tax bands:** 0%, 15%, 18%, 21%, 23%, 25%
- **Statutory deductions:** Pension (8%), NHIS, NHF
- **Consolidated Relief Allowance (CRA)** — ₦200,000 + 20% of gross income
- Auto-calculation of monthly and annual tax liability per employee
- Suitable for SMEs and finance teams managing payroll compliance

### 2. Payroll Processing Template
A complete payroll management template generating individual payslips with:
- Gross remuneration breakdown (basic, housing, transport, allowances)
- Statutory deductions (PAYE, Pension, NHIS, NSITF, NHF)
- Net pay computation
- Year-to-date tracking
- Designed for monthly payroll cycles across multiple employees

### 3. Vehicle Fueling Schedule (2026)
A fleet management tracker monitoring fuel consumption and costs across multiple vehicles:
- **Vehicles tracked:** Toyota Hilux, Toyota Corolla, Ford (company fleet)
- **Period:** 8-month tracking (January – August 2026)
- Per-vehicle fuel consumption and cost analysis
- Monthly expenditure summary for management reporting
- Flags unusual consumption patterns for audit purposes

### 4. Employee Leave Tracker
An annual leave management system tracking employee leave entitlements and usage:
- Leave type categorisation (annual, sick, maternity, compassionate)
- Balance tracking per employee
- Department-level leave calendar
- Compliance with Nigerian Labour Act leave entitlements (6 days minimum annual leave)
